চিঠিChithi

Write a letter, copy it, and paste it into any email. Nothing you write is uploaded.

A letter, not just another email

toChithi (চিঠি means “letter” in Bangla) turns what you type into a page from a ruled letter notebook: the margin line, the date in the corner, the greeting, your words and your sign-off. Copy it into Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo Mail or Apple Mail and it arrives as a letter, not a block of plain text. The person you write to needs nothing installed.

How it works

  1. Write. Format your words and add the date, a greeting, photos and a P.S.
  2. Choose the paper. Notebook, yellow pad, blue paper, pink pad, graph paper, airmail, plain, kraft or night, or design your own: lines, margin, edge, ink and lettering.
  3. Copy and paste into any email. You can also save the letter as a PNG image, or copy its HTML for a newsletter tool.

For every kind of letter

Start from a template: a personal letter, a thank-you note, an invitation or a formal application. Write in English or Bangla; dates and numerals follow the language you choose.

Private by design

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you write is uploaded, there is no account to make, and it is free. Your draft stays on your device; save it as a file to carry it to another computer. The source code is on GitHub.

Questions

Does the letter keep its look in Gmail and Outlook?

The letter is built from plain tables with inline styles, which email editors keep when you paste. Ruled lines are the most delicate part: where an email program drops them, the paper colour, margin line and layout remain. Airmail and plain paper use only colours and borders.

Can I send it as an image?

Yes. Make a PNG image of the letter and attach it, or share it anywhere a picture goes. An image also keeps handwritten lettering exactly as you see it.

Can I write in Bangla?

Yes. Switch the page to বাংলা: the sample letter, templates, date and numerals change with it.

Is it really free?

Yes, with no sign-up and no adverts. It is a static page with no server behind it, so there is nothing to pay for.
